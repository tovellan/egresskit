# Code of conduct

Project spaces are for constructive technical collaboration. Participants must be
respectful, specific, and considerate. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct,
deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
accepted.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate these rules and
may restrict participation when needed to protect the project community.

Report conduct concerns through a private security advisory with the subject marked as a
conduct report. Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Good-faith reports
will not result in retaliation.
